I am very interested in pseudo science topics. I watch content related to the flat Earth theory and pseudo-archaeology. On one hand, witnessing such foolishness isn't exactly pleasant; on the other, thanks to this content and the people who debunk such nonsense we can learn the actual facts and broaden our horizons.
That is how I discovered this book which was actually a decent read, even though the content is utter nonsense.

The author is a skeptic. He believes that centuries of research into the pyramids have failed to provide a definitive answer to such questions: "why were pyramids built in ancient Egypt?", "how they built it", "what was the purpose of this enormous effort?". In reality, the answer is simple and easily found, not to mention the hundreds of research papers on the subject produced over many years by thousands of scientists worldwide.

To the author, however, the fact that the pyramids are tombs is merely an unconfirmed theory. He views scientists as lazy and resistant to change their minds, unwilling to take into account alternative arguments. Christopher Dunn comes to the rescue, who claims that the Giza pyramid was used to generate electricity. I must admit that the author draws upon his engineering background to describe his on-site research in a thorough, technical manner. The sections detailing the experiments and technical aspects are convincing; he explains why the pyramid's architecture and materials make it resemble a power generation facility.
Everything falls apart, however, when Dunn moves to his conclusions. His message is absurd, perhaps even dangerous. There are more and more people who undermine scientific achievements without presenting their own evidence. One of them is Christopher Dunn, who didn’t stop with this book and continues to present and promote his theories to this day.

This is an absurd book. The only positive aspect is that it’s a decent read, even though it remains very technical for long stretches. I wasn’t convinced by the author’s theories, and I recommend that all skeptics stay away from this nonsense.
